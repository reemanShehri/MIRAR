Who we are

We don’t pitch the future. We ship it.

MIRAR is a team of five with one shared obsession: turning bold ideas into products that work in the real world. Technology, management, marketing and finance sit at the same table, so nothing we build is ever just a good idea. It’s designed, funded, branded and launched.

Our mix is unusual on purpose: a trainer with an English degree, a software engineer and AI trainer, a computer engineer and marketer, a telecom engineer who runs social content, and a lawyer and social entrepreneur. Different minds, one team.

We move fast, think in systems and care about the details people feel. Our work earned us a win at the YIH Hackathon with Kidora, one of our successful projects, and this is only the beginning.
